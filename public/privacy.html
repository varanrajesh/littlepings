Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

🎉 Short version: We collect absolutely nothing. No data, no cookies, no tracking.

Who we are

LittlePings (littlepings.com) is a free, browser-based keyboard-smashing toy designed for toddlers and babies aged 0–5.

Data we collect

None. LittlePings does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data whatsoever. This includes:

  • No names, email addresses, or account information
  • No device identifiers or IP addresses
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies or local tracking
  • No keystrokes or session recordings

Cookies

We do not use cookies. The app may store a small amount of data in your browser's localStorage to remember your chosen theme between sessions. This data never leaves your device.

Third-party services

LittlePings is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect standard server-side access logs (IP addresses, request paths) for security and abuse-prevention purposes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details. We do not have access to or control over these logs.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party tracking or advertising service.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

LittlePings is designed for young children but collects no information from anyone — children or adults. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). No parental consent is required because we collect zero personal information.

Ads

There are no advertisements of any kind on LittlePings.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "last updated" date above. Since we collect no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@littlepings.com.
