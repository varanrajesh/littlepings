LittlePings – Free Keyboard Toy for Toddlers and Babies

LittlePings is a free, safe, and fun keyboard-smashing toy designed for babies and toddlers aged 0 to 5 years. Every key press creates colourful animated emoji bubbles and plays a delightful piano-like ping sound. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases — just pure toddler joy.

Features

  • Colourful animated emoji bubbles on every key press
  • Playful piano-like ping sounds using the Web Audio API
  • 6 beautiful colour themes: Candy Land, Deep Ocean, Jungle, Outer Space, Sunset, and Arctic
  • Key smash counter with milestone confetti at 100, 500, 1000, and 2000 smashes
  • Parent lock — tap any corner 5 times to lock the app so toddlers cannot exit
  • Idle attract screen and session summary screen
  • Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile — tap the screen to create bubbles on touchscreen
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) — install to home screen and use offline
  • 100% free, zero ads, zero data collected, zero accounts required
  • Safe for kids — no external links, no tracking, no cookies

How to Use LittlePings

Simply open littlepings.com in any browser and hand the keyboard to your toddler. Every key they press makes a colourful bubble pop up on screen with a satisfying sound. On mobile or tablet, tap anywhere on the screen to create bubbles. Use the theme switcher in the top-right corner to change the colour theme. Activate the parent lock by tapping any corner of the screen 5 times quickly — enter PIN 1234 to unlock.

Why Parents Love LittlePings

Toddlers love keyboards. Instead of fighting it, LittlePings turns keyboard smashing into a safe, screen-time-friendly activity. There are no links to click, no ads to tap, no videos to autoplay, and no data being collected. It is a completely closed environment designed specifically for young children.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LittlePings free?

Yes, completely free. No ads, no accounts, and no in-app purchases — ever.

Is LittlePings safe for babies and toddlers?

Yes. There are no links, no ads, no external content, and no data collected. It is a closed keyboard-smashing toy designed specifically for babies and toddlers aged 0 to 5.

Does LittlePings work on mobile and tablets?

Yes. LittlePings works on any device with a browser — desktop, tablet, or mobile. Tap anywhere on a touchscreen to create bubbles.

Does LittlePings work offline?

Yes. LittlePings is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and works fully offline after the first visit. You can also install it to your home screen like a native app.

What colour themes does LittlePings have?

LittlePings has 6 colour themes: Candy Land, Deep Ocean, Jungle, Outer Space, Sunset, and Arctic — each with unique bubble colours, background gradients, and emoji sets.

What is the parent lock?

The parent lock lets you lock the app so toddlers cannot accidentally close it or navigate away. Tap any corner of the screen 5 times quickly to activate the lock. Enter PIN 1234 to unlock it.
